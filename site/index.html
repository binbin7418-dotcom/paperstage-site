Photo tips & privacy

Two minutes here makes a better film — and your photo stays yours.

A photo that cuts well

  • Full-body or three-quarter shot — your whole outfit in frame.
  • Face visible, looking toward the camera.
  • A plain-ish background helps the cut-out stay crisp.
  • Wear the outfit you want to be seen in — that's the fun part.
  • For a Duo, similar photo styles for both people cut best.
  • An ordinary phone photo is fine. Really.

What we do with it

  • Your photo is used only to make this one film.
  • We never sell it, post it, or use it to train anything.
  • Our working copy is deleted within 7 days of delivery.
  • Please only send photos of yourself, or ones you have the subject's permission to use.
  • The finished film is yours — post it anywhere, forever.
